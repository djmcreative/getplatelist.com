Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Email dakota@djm-creative.com. Platelist is made by DJM Creative in Minneapolis.

My sign-in code didn't arrive

Codes come from codes@platelist.djm-creative.com and usually arrive within a minute. Check your spam or promotions folder. If it's still missing, tap Send a new code after a minute, or sign in with Apple or Google instead.

How do I report something?

Open the dish, photo, note, list, restaurant or profile and tap Report. Reports go to Platelist, not to the person you reported.

How do I block someone?

Open their profile and tap Block. You'll stop seeing their ratings, notes and lists. To unblock, go to Profile → Blocked people.

A restaurant is missing or wrong

When you log a dish, you can add a restaurant that isn't listed. If a restaurant's details are wrong or it has closed, report the restaurant and we'll fix it.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile → Delete account. This permanently deletes your profile, ratings, notes, photos, lists and follows. If you signed in with Apple, Apple asks you to confirm, and Platelist is removed from your Apple ID. You can also ask us to delete it by email.
