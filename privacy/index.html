What other people can see

Platelist is a public place to share what you eat.

  • Anyone, even without an account: your username, display name, profile photo, ratings, notes, dish photos, and lists.
  • Signed-in members: also your Want to try list and who you follow.
  • Only you: your email address, your blocks, and the reports you send. Reports go to Platelist's admin, not to the person reported.
  • People you block can still see your public content, but you won't see theirs.

Location

Platelist asks for your location only while you're using the app, and only after explaining why. It's used for two things: listing the restaurants nearest you when you tap the plus button, and centering the map on you.

Your location is sent to our server to find nearby restaurants, rounded to about 10 meters, and isn't saved to your account or shown to anyone. You can turn location off in your phone's settings and still search restaurants by name.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and content until you delete them. Delete your account any time in Profile → Delete account. That permanently deletes your profile, email, ratings, notes, photos, lists, Want to try list, follows, blocks, and reports, right away.

Dishes and restaurants you added stay in the shared catalog without your name. Copies may remain briefly in our providers' backups and logs before they're overwritten.

Your choices and rights

  • Change or remove content: edit or remove your ratings and notes, delete lists, and change your photo in the app.
  • Delete everything: Profile → Delete account.
  • Location, camera, and photos: allow or turn them off any time in your phone's settings.
  • Access or correct your data: email dakota@djm-creative.com. Depending on where you live, you may have more rights, such as getting a copy of your data. We'll answer within 45 days.

Children. Platelist isn't for children under 13. If we learn a child under 13 made an account, we'll delete it. Parents can contact us at dakota@djm-creative.com.

Security. Data is encrypted in transit, and access to the database is limited by account. No system is perfectly secure, so use a secure email account.

Changes. We'll post updates here and tell you in the app about important changes.
