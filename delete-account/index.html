Delete your Platelist account

Platelist is made by DJM Creative. You can delete your account in the app at any time, or ask us to delete it for you.

In the app

Go to Profile → Delete account and confirm. Your account is deleted right away. If you signed in with Apple, Apple asks you to confirm, and Platelist is removed from your Apple ID.

Without the app

Email dakota@djm-creative.com from the email address you use for Platelist, with the subject Delete my Platelist account, and include your username. We'll delete the account within 30 days, usually much sooner, and reply to confirm.

What's deleted

Your profile, email address, ratings, notes, photos, lists, Want to try list, follows, blocks and reports are permanently deleted.

What stays

Dishes and restaurants you added stay in Platelist's shared catalog, without your name. Copies may remain in our providers' backups and logs for a short time before they're overwritten. More details are in the Privacy Policy.
